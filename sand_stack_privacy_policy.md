# Privacy Policy for Sand Stack: Colour Pour

**Last Updated:** September 17, 2026

Thank you for playing **Sand Stack: Colour Pour** ("we", "our", or "the Game"). Your privacy is deeply important to us. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure when you play our game on mobile devices, tablets, or web browsers.

---

## 1. Summary of Key Principles
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, require, or store personal information such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, or precise GPS location.
- **No Mandatory Account Registration:** You can play every level completely anonymously without creating an account or logging in.
- **Local On-Device Storage:** Your game progress, unlocked levels, stars, coins, high scores, and audio/accessibility preferences are stored locally on your device via standard browser or webview storage (`localStorage`).
- **Offline Capable:** The core puzzle gameplay operates fully offline without requiring an active internet connection.

---

## 2. Information We Store Locally
To provide seamless puzzle progression and remember your preferences, the Game stores the following keys locally on your device:
- **Game Progress:** Current level number, stars earned, total score, and completion stats.
- **Settings & Accessibility:** Sound effects (SFX) toggle, vibration/haptics toggle, animation speed, letter color indicators for color-blind accessibility, large text toggle, and dark/light theme choice.

This information never leaves your device and is not synchronized to external proprietary servers unless you manually back up your device.

---

## 3. Advertising & Analytics (If Applicable)
If optional rewarded video advertisements or non-personalized banner ads are enabled in the mobile distribution:
- Third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads) may collect non-personal device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / IDFA), IP addresses for coarse regional localization, and ad performance interaction metrics.
- These partners handle data in accordance with their respective privacy policies. You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking anytime in your device's operating system settings (Settings > Google > Ads on Android, or Settings > Privacy > Tracking on iOS).

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)
Sand Stack: Colour Pour is a family-friendly casual puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to delete any such data.

---

## 5. Security of Your Data
Because your progress and preferences are maintained directly on your local device storage, the security of your data is governed by your device's built-in operating system security and access controls.

---

## 6. Your Rights & Data Deletion
You retain complete control over your gameplay data at all times:
- You can reset your progress anytime inside the game by navigating to **Settings > Start over from level 1**.
- You can wipe all stored data completely by clearing your browser/app storage or uninstalling the Game.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may periodically update this policy to reflect enhancements to the Game or legal requirements. Updates will be posted directly to this URL with a revised "Last Updated" date.
